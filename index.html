About Us

Welcome to Kingsukh Guest House — your perfect retreat surrounded by nature and tranquility. Nestled beside the beautiful Barshal Water Tank in Barhanti, we offer cozy rooms, delicious meals, and warm hospitality that make every guest feel at home.
